Within-input paired effect of the Ridge-Bypass package (train-only ridge initialization plus trainable linear bypass and nonlinear gain 0.1). Signed relative change is 100*[RMSE(Ridge)-RMSE(Base)]/RMSE(Base) at the same N and training seed. Negative indicates lower error; positive indicates higher error. Points and bars show the mean and sample SD of three paired relative changes (not the ratio of seed means); open marks show all three changes. Each RMSE uses the same 41 validation wings; the 123 wing-seed combinations are not independent samples. The original no-bypass G0-Base N=10 is retained as a historical diagnostic comparator and remains on publication HOLD. Its near-constant behavior inflates that comparison; it is not the prior enhanced G0. This comparison does not isolate initialization, branch gain and bypass architecture as separate causal effects.
