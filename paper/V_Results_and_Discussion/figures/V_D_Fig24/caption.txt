Regional pressure-error changes (top; negative is improvement) and per-wing degradation fractions (bottom; larger is more frequent degradation) for paired knowledge increments. All 41 validation wings and three matched seeds are retained. Degradation is strictly positive paired RMSE change at zero tolerance; fractions are computed within each seed before averaging. Individual-wing deterioration is retained even if the overall mean improves. Root: eta<=0.20; Mid: 0.20<eta<0.80; Tip: eta>=0.80; Near tip: eta>=0.95 (a subset of Tip). All regions exclude LE0 and the tip cap. Seed-wing records are paired observations, not 123 independent samples. Regional membership uses float32 eta saved in the formal validation prediction NPZ, matching training evaluation.
