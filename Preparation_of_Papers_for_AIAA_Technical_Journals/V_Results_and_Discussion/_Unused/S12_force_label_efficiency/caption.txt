Validation RMSE in total lift, drag, and pitching moment at N=10, 20, 50, 100 target training wings. Small open symbols retain all three seeds without jitter; connected large symbols and bars show their mean and sample SD (ddof=1), not confidence intervals. Forty-one fixed validation wings are used per seed. Frozen predicted Cp, geometry and flow conditions feed separately fitted lightweight regression heads. These are total CFD coefficients, not forces obtained by pressure integration. Coefficient values are not rescaled. Insets enlarge the N=100 comparison using the same means and sample SD with categorical model positions; no additional results are introduced.
