Figure VE1. Mean absolute errors of (a) CL, (b) CD and (c) CMz on the same 88 previously exposed exploratory test wings. Each point is the mean of three independently sampled, nested training-subset sequences A/B/C; error bars are sample standard deviations (ddof=1), not confidence intervals. Initialization and data-order seeds are fixed. D0: geometry/flow only; D1: fixed-M2 predicted Cp; D2: true CFD Cp (Oracle). N counts downstream force-labelled training wings and excludes the prior supervision of the fixed M2 model. 1 drag count = 1e-4. Lines connect only the seven observed budgets, without smoothing or additional samples.
